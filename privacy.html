SEEN: WOULD YOU RATHER

Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026

Seen is a party game. We built it so your game nights stay yours: there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. This policy explains the small amount of data the app does handle and why.

“We” means ennhila, the developer of Seen. Contact us any time at ennhila.apps@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Player names, your custom questions, the decks you pick, your theme and other settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

What we and our service providers receive

DataWhyWho handles it
Purchase and subscription status, an anonymous app user ID, device type, country/currencyTo unlock the subscription, restore it on a new device, and offer trials and discounts you’re eligible forApple App Store / Google Play, and RevenueCat (our subscription service)
Crash reports: app version, device model, operating system, and the technical details of the errorTo find and fix bugs. Crash reports do not include names, questions or answersSentry (our crash reporting service)

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos, or what you pick in the game. We do not sell or share your data for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Payments are processed entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your card details.

Permissions

  • Notifications — only if you start a free trial, to remind you the day before it ends. The reminder is scheduled on your device.
  • Photos (add only) — only when you tap “Save image” to save your recap card. Seen cannot see your photo library.

Age

Seen is not directed at children under 13. The “After dark” deck is for adults only and is hidden until a player confirms they are 18 or older.

How long data is kept

On-device data stays until you delete it or the app. RevenueCat keeps purchase records for as long as needed to provide your subscription and meet tax and accounting rules. Sentry keeps crash reports for up to 90 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you can ask to access, correct or delete data linked to you, or object to how it’s used. Because we don’t have accounts, the simplest way is to email ennhila.apps@gmail.com; we’ll respond within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

International transfers

Our service providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

Changes

If we change this policy we’ll update the date above, and for significant changes we’ll let you know in the app.
