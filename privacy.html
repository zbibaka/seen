SEEN: WOULD YOU RATHER

Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

Seen is a party game. We built it so your game nights stay yours: there are no accounts and no ads inside the app. We do advertise Seen on other apps, and we measure those ads only as described below. This policy explains the small amount of data the app does handle and why.

“We” means ennhila, the developer of Seen. Contact us any time at ennhila.apps@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Player names, your custom questions, the decks you pick, your theme and other settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

What we and our service providers receive

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, precise location, photos, or what you pick in the game. We do not sell your data.

Ad measurement and your choices

  • EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland: ad measurement is off until you tap “Allow” when Seen asks. Our legal basis is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). If you don’t allow it, AppsFlyer is never started.
  • Everywhere else: ad measurement is on by default and you can turn it off any time.
  • iPhone: Seen only reads your advertising identifier if you choose “Allow” in Apple’s “Allow Seen to track your activity?” prompt. If you don’t, we only use Apple’s privacy-preserving attribution (SKAdNetwork and Apple Ads attribution), which does not identify you.
  • Turn it off: open the Seen menu → Ad measurement. This stops AppsFlyer on your device and stops linking your subscription to it. You can also withdraw tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

AppsFlyer’s own privacy policy: appsflyer.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Payments are processed entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your card details.

Permissions

  • Notifications — only if you start a free trial, to remind you the day before it ends. The reminder is scheduled on your device.
  • Photos (add only) — only when you tap “Save image” to save your recap card. Seen cannot see your photo library.

Age

Seen is not directed at children under 13. The “After dark” deck is for adults only and is hidden until a player confirms they are 18 or older.

How long data is kept

On-device data stays until you delete it or the app. RevenueCat keeps purchase records for as long as needed to provide your subscription and meet tax and accounting rules. Sentry keeps crash reports for up to 90 days. AppsFlyer keeps ad measurement data for as long as set in our AppsFlyer account and its standard retention terms; you can ask us to delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you can ask to access, correct or delete data linked to you, or object to how it’s used. Because we don’t have accounts, the simplest way is to email ennhila.apps@gmail.com; we’ll respond within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

International transfers

Our service providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

Changes

If we change this policy we’ll update the date above, and for significant changes we’ll let you know in the app.
